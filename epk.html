About the artist

Original songs. Country and rock influences.

Bob Running performs solo acoustic outlaw country and Americana, mixing original songs with selected covers. Northern Kentucky has been home for the past 30 years.

Bob started playing guitar as a teenager in Central Florida, but was making up songs before he knew how to play. He played in rock bands during the 1980s while continuing to play acoustic music. His influences include Johnny Cash, Waylon Jennings, Willie Nelson, Chris Stapleton, and Tyler Childers, along with Jimi Hendrix and Led Zeppelin.

His songs explore everyday life and hard times, often starting with an interesting phrase. “Corner Store” draws on memories of his childhood store, blending personal details with storytelling. Coming up with new musical ideas keeps him writing and playing.

The live show

Solo acoustic.
Originals and covers.

Bob Running performs outlaw country and Americana for taverns, breweries, restaurants, listening rooms, festivals, and private events.

  • Solo acoustic performance
  • One to three hour-long sets
  • 15-minute breaks between sets
  • Original music and selected covers
  • PA and microphone provided
  • Available within approximately 60 miles of Fort Thomas, Kentucky

Representative covers

  • “On the Road Again” — Willie Nelson
  • “Much Too Young (To Feel This Damn Old)” — Garth Brooks
  • “It Ain’t Easy Being Me” — Chris Knight
  • “Fire Away” — Chris Stapleton
  • “Pancho and Lefty” — Townes Van Zandt
